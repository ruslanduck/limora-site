03 Limora Connect

The CAPTURE ONE bridge

Connect sits alongside CAPTURE ONE and moves every frame into Limora as it's shot – no exports, no manual uploads.

  • Real-time naming – captures renamed on the fly to your SKU taxonomy.
  • Automated session setup – folder structures generated as each shoot begins.
  • Instant redundant backup – assets backed up in real time as shot.
  • In set – the day's shot list loads into the capture station, so every frame is matched to its SKU as it lands.
  • On location – the same naming and session rules apply offline, then sync when a connection returns.

What that means in practice

One premise: the platform should be shaped around the client, not the other way around. That shows up in three ways.

01

Completely customizable, by design

Not a fixed product with a menu of settings – a foundation configured to each client's exact requirements at deployment. Existing taxonomies, naming conventions and workflows don't get restructured to fit Limora. Limora gets built around them.

02

Fast to deploy, faster to learn

Because the system is shaped to match how a team already works, the training curve is short by construction. No months-long onboarding to translate your operation into someone else's platform logic – it speaks your operation's language from day one.

03

Specialized tools the big providers can't offer

A platform built for thousands of customers has to generalize. Limora doesn't. Every deployment can include the purpose-built tooling a client actually needs – tools that exist because a real client needed them, not because they tested well in a focus group.
